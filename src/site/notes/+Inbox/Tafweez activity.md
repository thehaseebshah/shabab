# Tafwid (Trusting Allah with Outcomes)

## Learning Objectives

- Distinguishing between human responsibility (*Asbab*) and Divine outcomes (*Nata'ij*), eliminating outcome-based anxiety, internalizing servitude through *Haq-e-Azmat-e-Ilahi*, and applying practical emotional detachment during stressful situations.

---

## Activity 1 (The Two Domains: Asbab vs. Nata'ij)

### Data

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Age Limit | 12+ |
| Group Size | 4–8 players per team |
| Duration | 20–25 minutes |
| Prep Time | 5 minutes |
| Difficulty | Easy to Medium |
| Category | Indoor, Critical Thinking, Tarbiyah, Mental Models |
| Items Needed | Whiteboard or paper, pens, scenario cards |
| Murabbi Rating | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### Setup

1. Draw two distinct zones on the board or chart paper:
   - **Zone 1: My Duty / Voluntary Actions (*Asbab / Apne Karne Ka Kaam*)**
   - **Zone 2: Allah's Domain / Non-Voluntary Outcomes (*Nata'ij / Ghair-Ikhtiyari*)**
2. Prepare scenario cards representing real-life stress situations.

### Scenario Cards

- **Scenario A (Exams / Studies):** Preparing for a crucial board exam or test.
- **Scenario B (Social Conflict):** Reaching out to an upset, hostile relative or friend.
- **Scenario C (Guiding Others):** Trying to get a younger sibling to wake up for Fajr.
- **Scenario D (Earning Sustenance):** Applying for jobs or running a small business.
- **Scenario E (Health Crisis):** Suffering from an illness or seeking medical treatment.

### How to Play

1. Divide Shabab into small teams and assign one scenario to each team.
2. Teams have 5 minutes to break down their scenario into two concrete lists:
   - Exactly what actions are **100% within their duty/capacity** (Zone 1).
   - Exactly what results belong **strictly to Allah's domain** (Zone 2).
3. Each team presents their breakdown.
4. **The "Shut-Up Call" Drill:** The Murabbi acts as the whispering *Nafs* / Anxiety (e.g., *"What if they reject your resume?"*, *"What if he insults you when you say Salam?"*).
5. The team must respond collectively with the Tafwid principle: *"My job is the effort; the outcome belongs to Allah."*

### Sample Answers for Murabbi

- **Scenario A (Exams):**
  - *My Duty:* Revision schedule, focused study, sleep on time, making Dua.
  - *Allah's Domain:* Exact marks, grade ranking, examiner's mood.
- **Scenario B (Hostile Relative):**
  - *My Duty:* Visit them, greet with Salam, offer a handshake, show adab.
  - *Allah's Domain:* Whether they smile, respond, or remain bitter. *(Reference: Maulana Zakariyya in Kandhla)*.
- **Scenario C (Fajr Call):**
  - *My Duty:* Gentle, persistent wake-up call (up to 3 times), encouraging words.
  - *Allah's Domain:* The opening of their heart and willingness to stand up.
- **Scenario D (Job/Business):**
  - *My Duty:* Skill refinement, ethical work, sending applications, punctual service.
  - *Allah's Domain:* Profit margins, market shifts, customer decisions.

### Murabbi Notes

- Emphasize that *Tafwid* is not laziness (*Tawakkul vs. Tawaakul*). Fulfilling the *Asbab* is a religious obligation.
- Stress that misery begins when we abandon our duty and obsess over the result.

## Activity 2 (The Tafwid Diagnostic Workshop)

### Data

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Age Limit | 13+ |
| Group Size | Individual reflection followed by small-group debrief |
| Duration | 20–25 minutes |
| Prep Time | 5 minutes |
| Difficulty | Medium |
| Category | Indoor, Spiritual Development, Emotional Resilience, Journaling |
| Items Needed | Blank sheets of paper / notebooks, pens |
| Murabbi Rating | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |

### Setup

Give each participant a blank sheet of paper and ensure privacy so they feel safe to write honestly without fear of being judged.

### How to Play

1. **Step 1 — The Worry Inventory (3 mins):**
   - Each participant silently writes down 2–3 current situations causing them stress, worry, or overthinking.
2. **Step 2 — Isolate the Desired Outcome (3 mins):**
   - Next to each worry, write the exact outcome they are anxiously trying to control or demand (*Marzi Ka Natijah*).
3. **Step 3 — Identify the Required Sunnah/Shariah Action (4 mins):**
   - Write down the single clear action or ethical responsibility (*Shari'at Ka Mutalibah*) Allah requires from them today regarding this issue.
4. **Step 4 — The Delegation Handover (5 mins):**
   - Draw a bold line across the "Desired Outcome" column.
   - Mentally and spiritually hand over that outcome to Allah through a brief silent Dua:
     > *"O Allah, I have done/will do my duty for Your sake. The outcome is in Your hands; I trust Your wisdom entirely."*
5. **Debrief (5 mins):** Murabbi leads a short group discussion on the feeling of relief (*Be-gham ho jana / Sukoon*) and the transition from *Talween* (struggling back-and-forth) to *Tamkeen* (firm habit).

### Key Discussion Points for Murabbi

- **Haq-e-Azmat-e-Ilahi:** We surrender to Allah not just for mental peace, but because He is our Master (*Aqa*) and we are His servants (*Ghulam*).
- **The "Heart of Ice" (*Barf Sa Dil*):** True resilience comes when external storms do not disturb internal conviction.
- **Correction vs. Ego:** When dealing with friends/family, correct for Allah's boundaries with gentleness; do not act out of wounded personal pride.

---

## Murabbi Reflection & Debrief Prompts

1. *"Why does obsessing over outcomes make us angry or depressed?"*
2. *"How did Maulana Zakariyya respond when his relative turned away, and why was he completely unbothered?"*
3. *"What is the difference between giving up (fatalism) and practicing Tafwid?"*
