### Ummah Innovators

> **Quick Summary:** In each round, 1 player acts as the **Judge (Ministry of Innovation)** and reveals a crisis/problem facing the community. All other players pick **2 Word Cards** from their hand, invent a creative device combining those 2 words, and give a 60-second pitch. The Judge awards 1 point to the best solution!

---

#### Materials & Setup

1. **Two Decks of Cards:**
   - **Problem Deck:** Challenges & dilemmas facing society (see sample list below).
   - **Word Deck:** Nouns, materials, mechanisms, and tools (see sample list below).
1. **Deal Hands:** Shuffle the Word Deck and deal **6 Word Cards** to each player. Place the Problem Deck face-down in the center.
2. **Choose First Judge:** The oldest or most enthusiastic player starts as the **Judge (Ministry Official)** for Round 1.

---

#### Step-by-Step Gameplay Flow

```
[1. Judge reveals Problem Card]
              ↓
[2. Players secretly choose 2 Word Cards from hand]
              ↓
[3. Each player gives a 60-second pitch]
              ↓
[4. Judge picks the winning invention (1 Point)]
              ↓
[5. Refill hands to 6 cards & pass Judge clockwise]
```

##### Step 1: The Problem Card (Judge's Turn)
The Judge draws the top card from the Problem Deck and reads it aloud:
> *Example: “Caravan travelers are getting lost and dehydrated during desert sandstorms.”*

##### Step 2: Combine 2 Word Cards (Players' Turn)
All players (except the Judge) look at their 6 cards and pick **2 cards** that can be creatively merged to solve the crisis.

##### Step 3: The 60-Second Pitch
Clockwise around the circle, each player reveals their 2 cards and pitches their invention:
- **Name your invention:** Combine the 2 words.
- **Explain the mechanism:** How do the 2 items work together?
- **Tell the benefit:** Why is this the most practical, clever, or life-saving solution?

##### Step 4: The Judge Decides
The Judge chooses their favorite pitch based on:
- **Creativity** (Clever use of unexpected words)
- **Feasibility & Logic** (Does it actually make sense to solve the problem?)
- **Persuasive Delivery** (Confidence, humor, and enthusiasm)

The winning player takes the Problem Card as **1 Point**.

##### Step 5: Draw & Rotate
- Players discard their 2 used cards and draw 2 new cards from the Word Deck to return their hand to 6.
- The player to the Judge’s left becomes the new Judge for the next round.
- **Winning:** The first player to collect **3 Problem Cards** wins the game!

---

#### Example Round Walkthrough (For Quick Understanding)

- **Problem Card Drawn:** *“Urgent medical supplies must cross high mountain valleys during winter storms.”*
- **Player Ali's Hand:** `[Kite, Honey, Copper, Falcon, Mirror, Drum]`
- **Ali selects 2 cards:** `Kite` + `Honey`
- **Ali's 60-Second Pitch:**
  > *“Honorable Minister! I present the **‘Kite Honey’**. Honey is an antibacterial natural medicine that never spoils in the cold. We pack it in lightweight ceramic pods attached to high-altitude kites. Using wind currents, the glider sails across impassable mountain peaks directly into isolated villages with zero risk to human life!”*
- **The Result:** The Judge is impressed by the logic and awards Ali the round!

#### Murabbi Coaching Notes

- **De-Westernized Framing:** Remind Shabab that Muslim polymaths like **Ismail al-Jazari** (father of robotics), **Ibn al-Haytham** (optics), and **Al-Zahrawi** (medical devices) were legendary innovators who combined physical principles to solve real communal problems.
- **Encourage Eloquence & Adab:** Remind players that a brilliant idea with a mumbled pitch loses to an average idea pitched with clarity, enthusiasm, and good speech (*Bayan*).
